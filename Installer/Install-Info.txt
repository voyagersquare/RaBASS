RaBASS - Analog Bass Instrument (Windows x64 / VST3)

Close Ableton Live and other DAWs before installing or updating.
The complete VST3 bundle is installed to:
C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\RaBASS.vst3

Ableton Live 12:
1. Open Settings > Plug-Ins.
2. Enable Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders, then click Rescan.
3. Find RaBASS in Browser > Plug-Ins and drag it onto a MIDI track.
4. Arm the track or create a MIDI clip. RaBASS generates its own audio.

This installer needs no Visual Studio, additional runtime download or internet
connection on the destination computer. An administrator account is required.
Remove RaBASS using Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps.
